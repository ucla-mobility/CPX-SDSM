Freshness factor

F penalises messages whose position information has gone stale in transit. Applied once per frame as \(R_{\text{eff}} = R \cdot F \cdot V\); it never modifies stored reputation. Staleness is measured not by latency itself but by how far the sender could have moved during it — its blind distance.

\[ d_{\text{blind}} = v \cdot \Delta t \qquad F = \max\!\left(F_{\text{floor}},\ \rho^{\,\max(0,\ d_{\text{blind}} - \text{grace})\,/\,D}\right) \]

A stationary sender (\(v = 0\)) is never penalised, however laggy the message. Otherwise F halves every \(D\) metres of blind distance past the grace band. Since \(\rho < 1\) and \(F_{\text{floor}} < R^*\), a sufficiently blind-distant sender always fails the gate regardless of reputation.

Constants mirror kinematic_freshness.py (GRACE_M, RHO_DIST, D_THRESHOLD_M, F_FLOOR); \(v\) comes from sender_motion.SenderMotionHistory and \(\Delta t\) from perception_message.send_latency_of. Timestamps are trusted inputs here — spoofing one is a separate concern (Stage 1b's kinematic checks).
